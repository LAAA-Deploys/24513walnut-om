The opportunity

Three residences. One infill site. A clear basis for value creation.

24513–24519 Walnut Street offers a rare three-unit foothold in the walkable core of Old Town Newhall: an occupied duplex, a detached rear house and direct access to the neighborhood’s dining, services and Metrolink station.

The investment combines current income with measurable rent upside. Future ADU or redevelopment potential may provide an additional path to value, subject to independent planning, design and legal review.

Old Town Newhall

Daily needs, Main Street and regional rail are close at hand.

The property sits near Old Town Newhall’s restaurant, arts and service core and approximately one-quarter mile from Newhall Metrolink Station. Current published schedules show 24 weekday trains stopping at Newhall.

  • Walkable Old Town retail and dining environment
  • Convenient access to Lyons Avenue and Interstate 5
  • Newhall Metrolink Station on the Antelope Valley Line
  • Established residential demand within the Santa Clarita Valley

Value-add highlight

Investigate ADU and longer-term site potential.

The 2025 Old Town Newhall Specific Plan applies Corridor zoning with a baseline maximum of three stories and 35 feet. Stacked residential in COR is generally tied to a vertical mixed-use commercial-building form. The site’s proximity to an existing rail station may also affect minimum-parking treatment under AB 2097.

No additional unit count, building area or entitlement is represented as approved. Buyers should confirm legal-nonconforming status, ADU eligibility, parking, setbacks, alley dedication and any development path directly with the City of Santa Clarita and qualified counsel.
