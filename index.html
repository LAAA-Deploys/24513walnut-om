Investment Overview

The Opportunity

Three Residential Units in Old Town Newhall

The LAAA Team of Marcus & Millichap is proud to present 24513–24519 Walnut Street, a three-unit residential investment in Old Town Newhall. Built in 1926 and 1954, the 2,364-square-foot property includes a duplex with two 2-bedroom/1-bath residences and a detached 1-bedroom/1-bath rear home on a 9,379-square-foot COR-zoned lot.

The investment offers 21.9% gross rent upside from reported monthly rents of $5,700 to broker pro forma of $6,950. The two duplex residences each generate $1,800 per month against a $2,400 pro forma, while the rear home generates $2,100 against a $2,150 pro forma. The three residences are fully occupied, subject to lease verification.

Newhall Metrolink Station is approximately 0.26 mile from the property, and Old Town Newhall’s Main Street district provides nearby dining, arts, library, and civic amenities. The lot’s alley access and COR zoning support independent ADU and site-planning study, with no entitlement represented. The offering is positioned for small-property investors, 1031 exchange buyers, and owner-operators seeking occupied income with defined rent upside.

Location Overview

Old Town Newhall

Historic Main Street with Regional Rail Access

Old Town Newhall combines Main Street dining, arts, and civic amenities within a compact historic district. The Old Town Newhall Library is approximately 0.09 mile from Walnut Street, The MAIN performance venue is 0.30 mile away, and William S. Hart Park is approximately 0.46 mile south.

Newhall Metrolink Station is approximately 0.26 mile from the property and serves the Antelope Valley Line, with 324 parking spaces listed by Metrolink. Interstate 5 access is approximately one mile west, and Henry Mayo Newhall Hospital is approximately 1.71 miles away.

Santa Clarita’s 2024 population estimate was 229,159. Census data reports median household income of $123,062, median gross rent of $2,544, and a 34.1-minute mean commute for 2020–2024. College of the Canyons reports 40,987 annual enrollment, adding a major education and employment anchor in the valley.

07 · Diligence and Disclosures

Buyer Due Diligence

Prospective purchasers should independently verify tenancy, physical condition, public records, operating expenses, and any development potential.

Income and tenancy

Obtain and review leases, deposits, payment history, tenancy dates, concessions, and current rent notices.

Physical condition

Inspect roofs, structure, electrical, plumbing, mechanical systems, drainage, parking, and all interior areas.

Public records

Confirm permitted unit count, bedroom configuration, square footage, legal-nonconforming status, and code history.

Development

Complete independent zoning, ADU, parking, title, utility, design, and entitlement analysis.

Zoning and Site Potential
