Investment Overview

The Opportunity

Three-Unit Residential Investment in Old Town Newhall

The LAAA Team of Marcus & Millichap is proud to present 24513–24519 Walnut Street, a fully occupied three-unit residential investment in Old Town Newhall. Built in 1926 and 1954, the 2,364-square-foot property comprises a duplex with two 2-bedroom/1-bath residences and a detached 1-bedroom/1-bath rear home on a 9,379-square-foot COR-zoned lot. Covered tenancies are subject to statewide AB 1482 rather than a local Santa Clarita multifamily rent stabilization program.

The investment case combines in-place income with 15.0% gross rent upside. Reported rents total $5,900 per month, while broker Pro Forma totals $6,785 per month. The selected $2,195 one-bedroom and $2,295 two-bedroom Pro Forma rents remain at or below the available survey benchmarks. Current rents and occupancy are subject to lease verification.

Newhall Metrolink Station is approximately 0.26 mile away, while Old Town Newhall's Main Street district provides dining, arts, library, and civic amenities within 0.46 mile. Alley access and COR zoning support independent ADU and site-planning study, with no entitlement represented. The offering is positioned for small-property investors, 1031 exchange buyers, and long-term holders seeking occupied income and site flexibility.

Location Overview

Old Town Newhall

Historic Main Street with Regional Rail Access

Old Town Newhall combines Main Street dining, arts, and civic amenities within a compact historic district. The Old Town Newhall Library is approximately 0.09 mile from Walnut Street, The MAIN performance venue is 0.30 mile away, and William S. Hart Park is approximately 0.46 mile south.

Newhall Metrolink Station is approximately 0.26 mile from the property and serves the Antelope Valley Line, with 324 parking spaces listed by Metrolink. Interstate 5 access is approximately one mile west, State Route 14 connects the Santa Clarita Valley and Antelope Valley, and Henry Mayo Newhall Hospital is approximately 1.71 miles away.

Santa Clarita's July 2025 population estimate is 228,430. Census data reports median household income of $123,062, median gross rent of $2,544, and a 34.1-minute mean commute for 2020–2024. College of the Canyons reports 40,987 students for the 2024–25 academic year, adding an education and employment anchor in the valley.
